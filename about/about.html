Who We Are

An IT Consulting, Product & Services Company

Wowoni Private Limited is an IT Consulting, Product, and Services company, started by professionals who have deep experience in core banking transformation, complex application development, enterprise architecture, infrastructure design and management, Big Data Solutions, and program & project management.

We are also partnered with PT MIDAS DAYA TEKNOLOGI, Jakarta, Indonesia and work hand in hand with deep knowledge and experience in implementing Oracle FLEXCUBE core banking, Which are Oracle FLEXCUBE Universal Banking Solution (FCUBS), Oracle FLEXCUBE Retail (FCR), Oracle FLEXCUBE Corporate (FCC), Oracle Banking Payments (OBPM), Oracle Banking Digital Experience (OBDX) etc.
