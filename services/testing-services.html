Strategic Testing Methodologies

A multi-faceted testing strategy helps provide broad, considered coverage across application behavior and quality attributes.

Evaluate user-facing behavior—including interfaces, workflows, and transactions—from the customer’s perspective to check for accurate, intuitive experiences.

Inspect internal code, logic, and design to assess financial calculations, data encryption, and error handling—important areas for security and integrity.

Use partial knowledge of internal systems to target testing of APIs, middleware, and complex integrations efficiently.

Apply skilled tester judgment to exploratory work, usability checks, ad-hoc scenarios, and User Acceptance Testing (UAT), helping identify subtle issues and review critical user journeys.

Validate that features behave as intended and assess performance, security, usability, reliability, and compliance as part of a wider view of application quality.
